| Bone springs | A bone with `r` or `t` and `se` or `re` in its metadata has physics. Each frame its tip `tp` is pulled towards last frame's tip (seen from the bone now, gravity `gd`·`m` taking off its tangential part) by `min(min(gap, 900·dt)·(1 − ri/100)·π/180, 1)` of the turn between them, added to a turn speed (a quaternion per sixtieth of a second); `se` springs back by `min(rs·π/180·dt, 1)` of a turn to rest and wraps the angles, `re` does neither; `lt` caps the speed at `ltmax`° per axis, `la` clamps the Euler angles and takes the excess off the speed, friction slerps `min(rf·dt, 1)` of the speed away; axes switch off only when `rax`/`ray`/`raz` are all given. Translation lags the scene movement by `1 − ti/100`, springs back by `ts` (`se`), falls by `gd·m·dt·1000`, loses `min(tf·dt, 1)` of its velocity, keeps locked axes (`tax`/`tay`/`taz`) and stays within `tm`·the layer's average scale. No step on the first frame | `wallpaper64.exe` physics step at `0x14020136e`–`0x140203648` (and its twin without layers), metadata parser `FUN_140265c30`, GLM helpers `FUN_140216070`, `FUN_1402167c0` | `physics.rs`, `quat.rs` | yes, SUBARU's ahoge swings behind the head; unit tests of spring, free, translation and gravity. ## Provenance status
- Our first code in `SceneRenderer/` was written while `RenderService/` was in the same repo. The
  two audits above compared our files against it and found nothing beyond common idioms.
  `RenderService/` is deleted now; it stays in git history (before the deletion commit) if a
  later audit needs it.
- Shader translator: the preprocessor and the rewrite rules are ours. The reserved-word table was
  compared with the GPL translator's tables (different structure, one word in common) and stays as
  it is: the words come from the GLSL 4.50 spec.
- The implicit-conversion fixer of `shader.rs` (plan item B7, 2026-10-22) was written without
  `RenderService/` open: the rules come from the HLSL language rules and from the shaders in our
  own asset tree, the patcher reads naga's own diagnostics. The `LightingV1` chunk is our own; the
  engine's chunk is a binary blob we do not have.
